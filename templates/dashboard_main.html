FAQ
How long does a campaign take?
Campaigns typically run 5–7 days. If the view target is not met, the campaign extends automatically until it is.
When does the views graph update?
Your views graph updates every time our team logs new data for your campaign. We update regularly during active campaigns.
When do top clips appear?
Only clips with 10,000+ views are shown. They appear automatically once they cross that threshold.
I forgot my password — what do I do?
Go to the login page and click "Forgot password?" — You can securely reset your password instantly using email verification.
How does the refund policy work?
20% of your budget is a non-refundable operations fee. The remaining 80% is your view guarantee — if views are not delivered after 30 days, you are eligible for a refund on undelivered views.
My views seem incorrect?
Contact us at magnetisemedia.co@gmail.com with your Campaign ID and we will investigate right away.
How long do clips stay live?
Clips remain live for a minimum of 2 months after campaign completion, continuing to accumulate organic views during this period.
